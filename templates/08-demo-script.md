# Demo script

**Length:** 12 minutes of demo, 18 of discussion. If you talk for 25 you have lost the room.

**0–1 · The problem, in their words.** Quote something the champion said in discovery.

**1–3 · The workflow today.** Show the painful version. Real screens if you can.

**3–9 · The new path.** One end-to-end task, start to finish. No feature tour.
Narrate what it is doing, not what it is.

**9–11 · Show it failing.** Deliberately. Then show the guardrail catching it.
This is the highest-trust moment in the whole meeting and almost nobody does it.

**11–12 · Numbers.** Latency, cost per task, current eval scores.

**Then stop talking.**

**Rules.** Never demo on live data you haven't rehearsed against. Have a recorded fallback.
If someone asks for a feature you don't have, say "we don't do that today" — then write it
down in front of them.
