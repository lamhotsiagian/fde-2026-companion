# First 90 days as an FDE

## Weeks 1–2 · Listening tour
Meet every named stakeholder. Ask each: what does good look like in 90 days; what has been
tried; what would you stop doing if you could.

Do the customer's actual job for at least one hour. Not a demo of it — the job.

**Ship one small useful thing by day 10.** A script, a dashboard, a cleaned dataset. Credibility
compounds from a shipped trivial thing far faster than from a good plan.

## Weeks 3–6 · First slice
Narrowest end-to-end path that proves the thesis. Golden set v1, built with a domain expert.
First eval numbers shared publicly, including the bad ones.

## Weeks 7–12 · Trust
Integrate into the tool people already use — never a new tab. Weekly eval review with the
customer. Written re-scope when reality diverges from the SOW, which it will.

## Day 90 · Review
Bring: the metric moved, the artifacts you left behind, the patterns you sent to product,
and one thing you got wrong and how you caught it.

## Warning signs you are losing the trust window
Meetings get shorter · your champion stops forwarding your updates · you hear about decisions
after they're made · someone else starts running "a quick parallel evaluation".
