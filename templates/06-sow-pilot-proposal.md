# Pilot proposal / SOW skeleton

**Parties · Term · Fee**

## 1. Objective
One sentence, in the customer's words, naming the workflow and the metric.

## 2. Scope — in
Specific. "Homeowners line of business, policy documents only, 50 named pilot users."

## 3. Scope — out
Longer than scope-in. This section is what stops the engagement from drifting.

## 4. Success criteria
| Metric | Baseline | Target | Measured by | Measured when |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Expansion criterion
Written before the pilot starts: hit the metric → proceed to phase 2 at [terms];
miss it → re-scope or stop. Agreeing this in advance is the single highest-value
paragraph in the document.

## 6. Customer obligations
Data access by [date] · named SME for ___ hours/week · environment provisioned by [date] ·
security review scheduled by [date]. **Each with a date, because slipped customer obligations
are the most common cause of a missed pilot deadline — and the hardest to raise later if you
never wrote them down.**

## 8. Change control
Any scope change is a written amendment. No exceptions, including friendly ones.
