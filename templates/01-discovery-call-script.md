# Discovery call script (45 minutes)

**0–3 · Frame.** "I want to understand the work before I propose anything. Is that OK?"

**3–15 · Workflow.**
- Walk me through the last time [the workflow] went wrong.
- Who downstream cares most about this?
- What does "done" look like for you, in numbers?
- How would you do this today if I gave you an extra person instead of software?

**15–30 · Stakeholders.**
- Who would resist this change, and why?
- Who has to approve — security, legal, compliance, IT?
- Who tried something similar before, and what did they learn?

**30–40 · Constraints.**
- What is the deadline, and what is driving it?
- What is the budget envelope? What are the gating approvals?
- Where does the data live? Who owns it? When was it last cleaned?

**40–45 · Close.** "Based on what I heard, here are the three things I'd want to test first —
does that match your priorities?"

**Within 2 hours, send:** what we agreed · what is open · what I am doing next.
