# Questions to ask them

The reverse interview is a scored round whether or not they tell you so.

**On the work**
- Walk me through the last engagement that went badly. What did the team change afterwards?
- What fraction of an FDE's time here is production code versus meetings?
- Who owns the system after the engagement ends?

**On the loop between field and product**
- Give me an example of a field pattern that became a product feature. How long did it take?
- Who decides when something gets productised?

*(If nobody can answer these two, the "product feedback loop" in the job description is decoration.)*

**On the bar**
- What separates a good FDE from a great one here, in behaviour rather than adjectives?
- Who was your last promotion to senior, and what did they do?

**On the reality**
- What is travel actually like — not the number in the posting, the number last quarter?
- What is on-call like for a customer-deployed system?
- Why is this role open?

**The one that gets an honest answer**
- What do you wish you'd known before you joined?
