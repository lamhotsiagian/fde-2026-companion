# Stakeholder map

| Role | Name | What they need | What they fear | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | | A quarterly win they can name | Spending on another failed pilot | Monthly |
| Champion | | To be right about backing you | Being embarrassed internally | Weekly |
| End user | | Their day to get easier | More work, or being replaced | Weekly during pilot |
| Blocker (security) | | Defensible controls | Being the one who approved the breach | At design, then at review |
| Blocker (legal / procurement) | | Standard paper, clear liability | Novel terms they must defend | Early, then at contract |
| Data owner | | Their data not misused | Being blamed for data quality | At ingest design |

**Rules.** The buyer and the user are different people with different definitions of success.
The blocker surfaces in week three, not at kickoff — go find them in week one. If you cannot
name your champion by day five, you do not have one.
