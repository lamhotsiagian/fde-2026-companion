# Market data — the volatile half of the book

**Last verified: 2026-05-06.**

Chapter 2 of the book cites public job listings and compensation ranges. Those URLs rot fast —
a Greenhouse or Lever req that is live today is a 404 in a quarter. Rather than print dead
links, the book points here, and this file is re-verified quarterly.

## How to re-run the search yourself

This is the durable skill; the table below is just this quarter's output.

1. **Company careers pages.** Search each for `forward deployed`, then again for
   `applied AI engineer`, `customer engineer`, `implementation engineer`,
   `solutions architect, AI`, and `technical deployment`. The title varies more than the job does.
2. **Aggregators.** Search the same terms on the major job boards, filtered to the last 30 days.
   Filter to postings in a salary-disclosure jurisdiction (CA, CO, NY, WA, IL) — those carry
   ranges by law.
3. **Cross-check compensation** against Levels.fyi at the same level and region. If the listing
   and the aggregator agree within ~15%, the number is defensible. If they disagree, trust the
   listing: it is legally disclosed, the aggregator is self-reported.
4. **Verify the role is really an FDE role** with the three-part test from Chapter 1: production
   code shipping, direct customer contact, product feedback loop. Two out of three is a
   mis-titled SE, MLE, or CE role.
